# Code of conduct

BetterLGTM follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and take feedback on the work rather than the person, and leave out harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/drmas). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, lock threads, or block people from the project, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
